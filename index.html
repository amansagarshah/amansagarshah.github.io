Current question Evolution / 001

Can evolution leave behind its own teacher?

Imagine a phenotype x that senses an object in the world. Its information changes the performance of a second phenotype y. If y eventually performs the task without x, reductive evolution may erase the very capacity that made the transition possible. What evidence would let us recognize such a history?

A hypothesis in formation, not a conclusion.

About

I study life by refusing to look at its pieces alone.

I’m Aman Shah, an independent researcher and writer from Gujarat. My formal training is in biotechnology; my work wanders through evolutionary biology, philosophy, mathematics, and literature.

I write to make questions precise enough to become dangerous to my own convictions. Some pieces are arguments. Some are field notes from an interior life. Their voices are allowed to differ.

“No fixed expectations—only the next honest question.”
